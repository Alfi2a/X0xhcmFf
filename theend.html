I wanted to say some (final) words
I know I wasn't the best version of myself after everything ended.
I guess I just couldn't bring myself to let go as easily as I wanted to, or perhaps as easily as I should have.
I didn't really know how to deal with everything I was feeling, and instead of handling it properly, I ended up doing things I probably shouldn't have.
I know I was stupid.
I spent so much of my energy trying to make you hate me, almost as if making myself someone you wouldn't want to remember would somehow make it easier for you to move on.
I thought that if I gave you enough reasons to dislike me, forgetting me wouldn't be so difficult.
Maybe, in some strange way, I thought I was making things easier for both of us.
But looking back, I realize that all I did was make things more complicated than they needed to be.
I let my emotions get the better of me, and I'm genuinely sorry if my actions hurt you, confused you, or left you with a bad impression of me.
That was never how I wanted things to end, even if my actions suggested otherwise.
Despite everything, I want you to know that I did enjoy the time we spent together.
I enjoyed the little moments, the conversations, the memories, and all the things that made that chapter of my life special.
And even though things didn't turn out the way I might have once hoped, I wouldn't say that I regret having experienced any of it.
I don't know how you look back on everything now.
Maybe it meant something to you, maybe it didn't mean as much, or perhaps you remember it completely differently.
I guess I'll never really know, and that's okay.
I just hope that, somewhere in your memories, there are still a few good things worth remembering.
I don't expect anything from you by saying all of this. I don't expect a reply, forgiveness, or even for you to feel the same way.
I simply wanted to acknowledge my mistakes and say the things I probably should have said a long time ago.
So, for the way I acted, for the things I said, and for making an already difficult situation even harder,
I'm truly sorry.
And despite how everything ended, I'm still glad our paths crossed.
I hope life has been treating you well.
And I genuinely wish you nothing but happiness, wherever life takes you.
After all —

I really am sorry.
